Bot avatars: the motion model is derived from bloub
https://github.com/jeremy-prt/bloub
The avatar engine in src/bot/ draws its eyes on a sphere and animates them the way bloub
does. These modules are ports or close adaptations of bloub's code:

  src/bot/sphere.ts    from src/bot/face.ts    (tangent-frame eye projection, blink squash,
                                                looped-noise drift, blink calendar)
  src/bot/face.ts      from src/bot/eyefit.ts  (the tabulated eye offset, solved once at
                                                load and interpolated, never per frame)
  src/bot/engine.ts    from src/bot/engine.ts  and src/bot/states.ts (dated setters, frozen
                                                on-screen pose for morphs, blended poses,
                                                masked glyph changes, one-shot states, the
                                                pointer `Look` with mix/spin/wander)

The look is Cubes' own: the eight silhouettes, twelve colours, eye-glyph vocabulary
(capsule, arc, line, dot, chevron) and the ten expressions come from this project, and every
measured constant was re-tuned for a 100-unit viewBox with R = 38. No bloub artwork,
palette or expression set is included.
